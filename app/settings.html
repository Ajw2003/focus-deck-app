    How it works

    How you pick a project and a category on the Focus screen. Every picker in the app follows this. Arrow keys and Enter always work on a computer.

    Choosing a card

    A task's due chip turns yellow, then red, as its time runs down. The cut-offs are a share of the time between setting the due date and the due date itself, so a task due in three months and a task due in three days both turn at a fitting moment. A task past its date shows amber “Past its date” unless you choose red “Overdue” under Comfort. A task can still pick its own lead times when you set its date.

    When you sort a thought into a project or edit a task, you see that project’s own categories first. The rest stay one tap away (“More categories”, “Show all”), and a project with none yet shows them all. Synced.
