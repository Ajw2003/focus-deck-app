Five tabs, one for each step

Each tab answers one question, in the order you use them. The grey boxes are today's screens, shown under the tab they move to.

1Capture

What's on my mind?
Jot a thought or add a task, then file it to a project.
JotterSort

2Plan

What still matters, and in what order?
Tidy up, Triage, then Roadmap. Roadmap is where projects live now.
Tidy upTriageRoadmapProjectsBoard: Up next

3Start

What should I do now?
Three suggestions from Triage, or 'Choose it myself'.
Start here

4Focus

What am I doing?
The carousels and today's task card, plus everything already in progress.
FocusBoard: In progress

5Review

Is it really done?
Check what's waiting for review, and see what you finished.
Board: DoneNeeds review label

Settings stays out of the tabs: on a phone it's the gear at the top, and on desktop it's at the bottom of the side menu. The All / Area / Project switch sits at the top of every tab.

The Board's three columns split across Plan, Focus and Review, so nothing is shown twice
