Your pick

Low light: a desk under a lamp

Everything on screen is a real thing people already use to keep track. Your one task is a sticky note in the lamp's pool of light. Categories are index cards and projects are manila folders, each kept in a wallet you flip through like CD sleeves. You jot thoughts on a notepad, stamp a task done with a rubber stamp, and slip a note back into the deck when it isn't the right one. Light mode is the same desk in daylight.

  • The folder facing you narrows the index cards underneath to that project, and their counts update to match.
  • Swipe or drag sideways to flip. On a computer, click a wallet and use the arrow keys, then press Enter to choose.
  • All three ways of choosing a card stay in the app as a setting. Try them with the switch below. Drawing, stamping and slipping back all really work.

Direction A

Index card

The "deck" taken literally: your focus task is a real ruled index card lying on top of the pile. Calm cool-blue desk, one red margin line as the only loud colour.

  • Body type is Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for readers with low vision. It's easy on tired eyes and on dyslexic readers.
  • Labels become coloured card tabs. Light mode first, with a dark "desk at night" version.

Direction B

Low light

Low-stimulus, dark-first. The screen is almost empty and the one task gets a soft amber glow. The other choices sit back as quiet outlines. Made to calm you down rather than wind you up.

  • Soft serif headings (Young Serif) with a clean sans for everything else. Very little motion: one slow "breathe" ring for starting a task.
  • Dark mode is the main look, with a dusk-paper light version.

Direction C

The deal

Bold and playful: labels are playing cards in strong colours with chunky ink outlines, and picking a task feels like drawing from a hand. Gives you a bit of a push, which might suit students in the school pilot.

  • Condensed Archivo in heavy weights for headings, the same family for body text. Flat colour, hard offset shadows, cards tilted a little.
  • Light mode first. The dark version swaps the paper for charcoal and keeps the colours.
